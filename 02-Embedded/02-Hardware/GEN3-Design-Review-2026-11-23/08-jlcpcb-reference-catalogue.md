## Interpretation and source limits

Fresh successful API matching verifies the catalogue fields above, **not** a placed order, actual reserved quantity, vendor shipping eligibility, footprint, pinout or electrical compatibility. In particular C272410 is an axial diode mismatch, the camera connector candidates are conditional, and the original TE connector remains unavailable. The removed audio items are listed for traceability, not to repopulate the design. C160404 demonstrates why a positive stock field alone is insufficient. The original gauge suffix is incomplete in Eagle; C184366 is a family comparison, not an exact source-orderable match.

The three BOM CSVs carry the snapshot classification for resolved codes. The 46-placement baseline intentionally preserves unresolved original part choices rather than silently assigning new MPNs. Recheck all references at design freeze.
